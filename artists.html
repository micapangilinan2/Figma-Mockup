FIFTEEN ARTISTS IN HISTORY

Here are 15 famous artists in history:

Scroll Down ↓

Leonardo da Vinci

  • Leonardo di ser Piero da Vinci (April 15, 1452 - May 2 1519), an Italian Polymath of the High Renaissance.
  • He was an active painter, theorist, sculptor, and architect and innovated ways of creating striking compositions.
  • Famous Artworks: Mona Lisa (c. 1503-1516), The Last Supper (1498), Lady with an Ermine (1489-91)

Michaelangelo

  • Michelangelo di Lodovico Buonarroti Simoni (March 6, 1475 - February 1564), an Italian sculptor, painter, architect and poet of the High Renaissance.
  • He was named as an achetypal Renaissance man for his creativeness and mastery in a range of artistic arenas.
  • Famous Artworks: David (1501-4), Pieta (1498-99), The creation of Adam (c. 1511)

Vermeer

  • Johannes Vermeer (October 31, 1632 - December 15, 1675), also known as ‘Jan Vermeer’, a Dutch painter who specializes in domestic interior scenes of middle class life.
  • His arts were forgotten for two centuries until it was eventually discovered again in the 19th century.
  • Famous Artworks: Girl with a Pearl Earring (1665)

Claude Monet

  • Claude Monet (November 14, 1840 – December 5, 1926), was a French painter and one of the founders of Impressionism.
  • He became known for capturing light, color, and changing moments in nature through loose and visible brushstrokes.
  • Famous Artwork: Impression, Sunrise (1872), Water Lilies, Haystacks (1890-91), Rouen Cathedral Series (1892-94)

Vincent van Gogh

  • Vincent van Gogh (March 30, 1853 – July 29, 1890), was a Dutch Post-Impressionist painter known for his expressive use of color.
  • His bold brushstrokes and emotional style greatly influenced the development of modern art.
  • Famous Artwork: The Starry Night (1889), Sunflowers (1888–1889), The Bedroom (1888)

Pablo Picasso

  • Pablo Picasso (October 25, 1881 – April 8, 1973), was a Spanish artist and one of the most influential artists of the 20th century.
  • He helped pioneer Cubism and experimented with many different styles throughout his artistic career.
  • Famous Artwork: Guernica (1937), Les Demoiselles d'Avignon (1907), The Weeping Woman (1937)

Gustav Klimt

  • Gustav Klimt (July 14, 1862 – February 6, 1918), was an Austrian painter known for Symbolism and the Vienna Secession movement.
  • His works are recognized for their decorative patterns, rich colors, and use of gold.
  • Famous Artwork: The Kiss (1907–1908), Portrait of Adele Bloch-Bauer I (1907), Judith I (1901)

J.M.W. Turner

  • J. M. W. Turner (April 23, 1775 – December 19, 1851), was an English Romantic painter known for his landscapes and seascapes.
  • His dramatic use of light, atmosphere, and color made him one of the most important landscape painters in history.
  • Famous Artwork: The Fighting Temeraire (1839), Rain, Steam and Speed (1844), The Slave Ship (1840)

Paul Cezanne

  • Paul Cézanne (January 19, 1839 – October 22, 1906), was a French Post-Impressionist painter.
  • His unique approach to form and structure influenced the development of modern art and later movements such as Cubism.
  • Famous Artworks: The Card Players (1890–1895), The Basket of Apples (c. 1893), and Mont Sainte-Victoire (series)

Raphael

  • Raphael (April 6, 1483 – April 6, 1520), was an Italian painter and architect of the High Renaissance.
  • He was admired for his balanced compositions and harmonious depictions of human figures.
  • Famous Artworks: The School of Athens (1509–1511), Sistine Madonna (1512), and The Transfiguration (1516–1520)

Rembrandt

  • Rembrandt (July 15, 1606 – October 4, 1669), was a Dutch painter and one of the greatest artists of the Dutch Golden Age.
  • He became famous for his mastery of light and shadow and his deeply expressive portraits.
  • Famous Artworks: The Night Watch (1642), The Anatomy Lesson of Dr. Nicolaes Tulp (1632), and The Return of the Prodigal Son (c. 1669)

Mary Cassatt

  • Mary Cassatt (May 22, 1844 – June 14, 1926), was an American painter and printmaker associated with Impressionism.
  • She was especially known for her paintings depicting women, children, and everyday family life.
  • Famous Artworks: The Child's Bath (1893), Little Girl in a Blue Armchair (1878), and The Boating Party (1893–1894)

Jackson Pollock

  • Jackson Pollock (January 28, 1912 – August 11, 1956), was an American painter and a major figure in Abstract Expressionism.
  • He became famous for his innovative drip painting technique and energetic abstract compositions.
  • Famous Artworks: No. 5, 1948 (1948), Blue Poles (1952), and Mural (1943)

Georgia O'Keeffe

  • Georgia O'Keeffe (November 15, 1887 – March 6, 1986), was an American modernist painter known for her paintings of flowers and landscapes.
  • Her distinctive style made her one of the most important figures in American modern art.
  • Famous Artworks: Jimson Weed/White Flower No. 1 (1932), Black Iris III (1926), and Cow's Skull: Red, White, and Blue (1931)

Edvard Munch

  • Edvard Munch (December 12, 1863 – January 23, 1944), was a Norwegian painter associated with Expressionism.
  • His emotionally intense works explored themes such as anxiety, love, and human experience.
  • Famous Artworks: The Scream (1893), Madonna (1894–1895), and The Sick Child (1885–1886)
